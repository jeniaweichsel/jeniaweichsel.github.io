Artist Statement

I came to painting from aerospace engineering, and I still think of a portrait as a structure held in quiet balance.

My figures are still, but not empty. A slight shift in gaze or gesture, a controlled surface, a softened edge: these carry the tension beneath the calm.

Shaped by icon painting, early Flemish painting, and Renaissance portraiture, I hold a whole inner life within quiet, ambiguous forms, where a single displaced detail can open the composition to feeling.
